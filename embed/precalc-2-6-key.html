Rational functions and their graphs

Due Thu 11/5, 2:00 PM34 problems

How it works

  • Domain: throw out every x that makes the bottom 0.
  • Factor, then cancel. A factor that cancels makes a hole (an open circle). A factor left on the bottom makes a vertical asymptote, a wall the graph never touches.
  • Horizontal asymptote: compare the highest powers.
    • Bottom power bigger: \(y=0\)
    • Same power: divide the leading numbers
    • Top power bigger: no horizontal asymptote. If it's bigger by exactly 1, divide; the answer without the remainder is the slant asymptote.
  • \(x\to1^-\) means x creeping up to 1 from the left. \(x\to1^+\) means from the right.
  • Graphing in 7 steps: symmetry, y-intercept (plug in 0), x-intercepts (top = 0), vertical asymptotes, horizontal asymptote, a few points, draw.

93
\(\dfrac{1-\dfrac{3}{x+2}}{1+\dfrac{1}{x-2}}\)

Write the equation for f

\[\begin{aligned} \text{Banned:}\quad & x+2=0\Rightarrow x=-2,\qquad x-2=0\Rightarrow x=2\\[8pt] \text{Top:}\quad & 1-\frac{3}{x+2}=\frac{x+2}{x+2}-\frac{3}{x+2}=\frac{x-1}{x+2}\\[8pt] \text{Bottom:}\quad & 1+\frac{1}{x-2}=\frac{x-2}{x-2}+\frac{1}{x-2}=\frac{x-1}{x-2}\qquad(x\ne1)\\[8pt] \text{Flip:}\quad & \frac{x-1}{x+2}\div\frac{x-1}{x-2}=\frac{x-1}{x+2}\cdot\frac{x-2}{x-1}\\[8pt] \text{Cancel:}\quad & f(x)=\frac{x-2}{x+2},\qquad x\ne-2,\ 1,\ 2 \end{aligned}\]

Graph it

\[\begin{aligned} \text{Symmetry:}\quad & f(-x)=\frac{-x-2}{-x+2}\ne f(x)\ \text{or}\ {-f(x)}\Rightarrow\text{none}\\[8pt] \text{y-int:}\quad & f(0)=\frac{0-2}{0+2}=-1\Rightarrow(0,-1)\\[8pt] \text{x-int:}\quad & x-2=0\Rightarrow x=2\ \text{(banned, so a hole, no x-int)}\\[8pt] \text{VA:}\quad & x+2=0\Rightarrow x=-2\\[8pt] \text{HA:}\quad & \frac{1x}{1x}\Rightarrow y=1\\[8pt] \text{Holes:}\quad & f(1)=\frac{1-2}{1+2}=-\frac13\Rightarrow\left(1,-\tfrac13\right)\\[8pt] & f(2)=\frac{2-2}{2+2}=0\Rightarrow(2,0)\\[8pt] \text{Points:}\quad & f(-4)=\frac{-6}{-2}=3,\quad f(-3)=\frac{-5}{-1}=5\\[8pt] & f(-1)=\frac{-3}{1}=-3,\quad f(4)=\frac{2}{6}=\frac13 \end{aligned}\]
Answer
Equation
\(f(x)=\dfrac{x-2}{x+2}\), with \(x\ne1\) and \(x\ne2\)
Vertical asymptote
\(x=-2\)
Horizontal asymptote
\(y=1\)
y-intercept
\((0,-1)\)
x-intercept
None (\(x=2\) is a hole)
Holes
\(\left(1,-\tfrac13\right)\) and \((2,0)\), drawn as open circles
94
\(\dfrac{x-\dfrac1x}{x+\dfrac1x}\)

Write the equation for f

\[\begin{aligned} \text{Banned:}\quad & \frac1x\Rightarrow x\ne0\\[8pt] \text{Times }\tfrac{x}{x}\text{:}\quad & \frac{x\cdot x-x\cdot\frac1x}{x\cdot x+x\cdot\frac1x}=\frac{x^2-1}{x^2+1}\\[8pt] \text{So:}\quad & f(x)=\frac{x^2-1}{x^2+1},\qquad x\ne0 \end{aligned}\]

Graph it

\[\begin{aligned} \text{Symmetry:}\quad & f(-x)=\frac{(-x)^2-1}{(-x)^2+1}=\frac{x^2-1}{x^2+1}=f(x)\Rightarrow\text{y-axis}\\[8pt] \text{y-int:}\quad & x=0\ \text{banned}\Rightarrow\text{none}\\[8pt] \text{Hole:}\quad & \frac{0^2-1}{0^2+1}=-1\Rightarrow(0,-1)\\[8pt] \text{x-int:}\quad & x^2-1=0\Rightarrow(x-1)(x+1)=0\Rightarrow x=\pm1\\[8pt] \text{VA:}\quad & x^2+1=0\Rightarrow x^2=-1\Rightarrow\text{none}\\[8pt] \text{HA:}\quad & \frac{1x^2}{1x^2}\Rightarrow y=1\\[8pt] \text{Points:}\quad & f(\pm2)=\frac{4-1}{4+1}=\frac35,\quad f(\pm3)=\frac{9-1}{9+1}=\frac45,\quad f(0.5)=\frac{-0.75}{1.25}=-0.6 \end{aligned}\]
Answer
Equation
\(f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-1}{x^2+1}\), with \(x\ne0\)
Symmetry
Mirrors across the y-axis
x-intercepts
\((-1,0)\) and \((1,0)\)
y-intercept
None (hole at \((0,-1)\))
Asymptotes
Horizontal \(y=1\). No vertical asymptote.
Hole
\((0,-1)\), drawn as an open circle
