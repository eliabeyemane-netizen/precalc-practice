Imaginary numbers

1
Write in standard form: \(\sqrt{-8}\cdot\sqrt{-2}+(3-2i)^2\)
5 pts
\[\begin{aligned} \sqrt{-8}&=\ub{\sqrt{-1}}{i}\cdot\ub{\sqrt{4\cdot2}}{2\sqrt2}=2i\sqrt2\\ \sqrt{-2}&=i\sqrt2\\ (2i\sqrt2)(i\sqrt2)&=2\,\hl{i^2}\,\ub{\sqrt2\cdot\sqrt2}{2}=2(\hl{-1})(2)=-4\\[4pt] (3-2i)(3-2i)&=\ub{3\cdot3}{9}+\ub{3(-2i)}{-6i}+\ub{(-2i)(3)}{-6i}+\ub{(-2i)(-2i)}{4i^2}\\ &=9\ \ub{-6i-6i}{-12i}+4(\hl{-1})=\ub{9-4}{5}-12i\\[4pt] -4+5-12i&=\ub{-4+5}{1}-12i \end{aligned}\]

Answer\(1-12i\)

2
Write in standard form: \(\dfrac{2+3i}{3-i}+i^{15}\)
5 pts
\[\begin{aligned} 15\div4&=3\ \text{R}\ \hl{3}\ \to\ i^{15}=i^{\hl3}=-i\\[4pt] \frac{2+3i}{3-i}\cdot\hl{\frac{3+i}{3+i}}&=\frac{(2+3i)(3+i)}{(3-i)(3+i)}\\ (2+3i)(3+i)&=\ub{6}{2\cdot3}+\ub{2i}{2\cdot i}+\ub{9i}{3i\cdot3}+\ub{3i^2}{3i\cdot i}=6+11i-3=3+11i\\ (3-i)(3+i)&=3^2+1^2=10\\[4pt] \frac{3+11i}{10}-i&=\frac{3}{10}+\frac{11}{10}i-\frac{10}{10}i=\frac{3}{10}+\frac{1}{10}i \end{aligned}\]

Answer\(\tfrac{3}{10}+\tfrac{1}{10}i\)

Inequalities

3
Solve and graph on a number line. Use interval notation: \(x^3+3x^2\ge4x+12\)
5 pts
\[\begin{aligned} x^3+3x^2\hl{-4x-12}&\ge0\\ \ub{x^3+3x^2}{x^2(x+3)}\ \ub{-4x-12}{-4(x+3)}&\ge0\\ (x+3)(\ub{x^2-4}{(x-2)(x+2)})&\ge0\\ x&=-3,\ -2,\ 2\\[4pt] (\vl{-4}+3)(\vl{-4}-2)(\vl{-4}+2)&=(-1)(-6)(-2)=-12\lt0\\ (\vl{-2.5}+3)(\vl{-2.5}-2)(\vl{-2.5}+2)&=(0.5)(-4.5)(-0.5)=1.125\gt0\\ (\vl0+3)(\vl0-2)(\vl0+2)&=(3)(-2)(2)=-12\lt0\\ (\vl3+3)(\vl3-2)(\vl3+2)&=(6)(1)(5)=30\gt0 \end{aligned}\]

Answer\([-3,-2]\cup[2,\infty)\)

4
Solve and graph on a number line. Use interval notation: \(\dfrac{3}{x+2}\ge\dfrac{1}{x-2}\)
5 pts
\[\begin{aligned} \frac{3}{x+2}\hl{-\frac{1}{x-2}}&\ge0\\ \frac{3(x-2)-1(x+2)}{(x+2)(x-2)}&\ge0\\ \frac{\ub{3x-6-x-2}{2x-8}}{(x+2)(x-2)}&\ge0\\ 2x-8=0\ \to\ 2x=8\ \to\ x&=4\ (\bullet)\\ x&=-2,\ 2\ (\circ)\\[4pt] \frac{2(\vl{-3})-8}{(\vl{-3}+2)(\vl{-3}-2)}&=\frac{-14}{(-1)(-5)}=\frac{-14}{5}\lt0\\ \frac{2(\vl0)-8}{(\vl0+2)(\vl0-2)}&=\frac{-8}{-4}=2\gt0\\ \frac{2(\vl3)-8}{(\vl3+2)(\vl3-2)}&=\frac{-2}{5}\lt0\\ \frac{2(\vl5)-8}{(\vl5+2)(\vl5-2)}&=\frac{2}{21}\gt0 \end{aligned}\]

Answer\((-2,2)\cup[4,\infty)\)

Asymptotes, holes and domain

5
Find all vertical, horizontal and slant asymptotes and any holes: \(f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-x-6}{x^3-9x}\)
5 pts
\[\begin{aligned} \hl{-3}\cdot\hl2=-6,\ \ \hl{-3}+\hl2=-1\ &\to\ x^2-x-6=(x-3)(x+2)\\ x^3-9x=x(\ub{x^2-9}{(x-3)(x+3)})&=x(x-3)(x+3)\\ f(x)&=\frac{\hl{(x-3)}(x+2)}{x\,\hl{(x-3)}(x+3)}=\frac{x+2}{x(x+3)},\ \ x\ne3\\[4pt] \text{Hole}:\ \frac{\vl3+2}{\vl3(\vl3+3)}&=\frac{5}{18}\ \to\ \left(3,\tfrac{5}{18}\right)\\ \text{VA}:\ x(x+3)=0\ &\to\ x=0,\ x=-3\\ \text{HA}:\ 2\lt3\ &\to\ y=0\\ \text{Slant}&:\ \text{none} \end{aligned}\]

AnswerVA \(x=0,\ x=-3\); HA \(y=0\); slant none; hole \(\left(3,\tfrac{5}{18}\right)\)

6
Find all vertical, horizontal and slant asymptotes and any holes: \(f(x)=\dfrac{2x^3+x^2+x-4}{x^2+x-2}\)
5 pts
\[\begin{aligned} x^2+x-2&=(x+2)(x-1)\\ \text{Top at }\vl1:\ 2+1+1-4&=0\ \to\ (x-1)\ \text{is on top too}\\[4pt] \frac{\hl{2x^3}}{x}=2x^2\ &\to\ 2x^3-2x^2\ \to\ \ub{x^2+2x^2}{3x^2}+x\\ \frac{\hl{3x^2}}{x}=3x\ &\to\ 3x^2-3x\ \to\ \ub{x+3x}{4x}-4\\ \frac{\hl{4x}}{x}=4\ &\to\ 4x-4\ \to\ 0\\ f(x)&=\frac{\hl{(x-1)}(2x^2+3x+4)}{\hl{(x-1)}(x+2)}=\frac{2x^2+3x+4}{x+2},\ \ x\ne1\\[4pt] \text{Hole}:\ \frac{2+3+4}{1+2}&=\frac93=3\ \to\ (1,3)\\ \text{VA}&:\ x=-2\\ \text{HA}:\ 2\gt1&\ \to\ \text{none}\\[4pt] \frac{\hl{2x^2}}{x}=2x\ &\to\ 2x^2+4x\ \to\ \ub{3x-4x}{-x}+4\\ \frac{\hl{-x}}{x}=-1\ &\to\ -x-2\ \to\ \ub{4+2}{6}\\ \text{Slant}&:\ y=2x-1 \end{aligned}\]

AnswerVA \(x=-2\); HA none; slant \(y=2x-1\); hole \((1,3)\)

7
Find the domain in interval notation: \(f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-4}{x^3-2x^2-x+2}\)
5 pts
\[\begin{aligned} \ub{x^3-2x^2}{x^2(x-2)}\ \ub{-x+2}{-1(x-2)}&=0\\ (x-2)(\ub{x^2-1}{(x-1)(x+1)})&=0\\ x&=2,\ 1,\ -1 \end{aligned}\]

Answer\((-\infty,-1)\cup(-1,1)\cup(1,2)\cup(2,\infty)\)

Solving polynomials

8
For \(f(x)=3x^3+2x^2-19x+6\): (a) list all possible rational zeros, (b) find all zeros.
5 pts
\[\begin{aligned} p:\ \pm1,\pm2,\pm3,\pm6\quad q:\ \pm1,\pm3\ &\to\ \pm1,\pm2,\pm3,\pm6,\pm\tfrac13,\pm\tfrac23\\ f(\vl2)&=\ub{24+8}{32}\ \ub{-38+6}{-32}=0\ \checkmark\\[4pt] \frac{\hl{3x^3}}{x}=3x^2\ &\to\ 3x^3-6x^2\ \to\ \ub{2x^2+6x^2}{8x^2}-19x\\ \frac{\hl{8x^2}}{x}=8x\ &\to\ 8x^2-16x\ \to\ \ub{-19x+16x}{-3x}+6\\ \frac{\hl{-3x}}{x}=-3\ &\to\ -3x+6\ \to\ 0\\[4pt] 3(-3)=-9:\ \ \hl9\cdot(\hl{-1})=-9,\ \ \hl9+(\hl{-1})&=8\\ 3x^2+\hl{9x-x}-3&=\ub{3x^2+9x}{3x(x+3)}\ \ub{-x-3}{-1(x+3)}=(3x-1)(x+3)\\ x&=\tfrac13,\ -3 \end{aligned}\]

Answer(a) \(\pm1,\pm2,\pm3,\pm6,\pm\tfrac13,\pm\tfrac23\) · (b) \(2,\ \tfrac13,\ -3\)

9
Given that \(1+2i\) is a zero, find all zeros of \(f(x)=x^3-5x^2+11x-15\).
5 pts
\[\begin{aligned} 1+2i\ &\to\ 1-2i\\ \big((x-1)-2i\big)\big((x-1)+2i\big)&=(x-1)^2-4\hl{i^2}\\ &=\ub{x^2-2x+1}{(x-1)^2}+4=x^2-2x+5\\[4pt] \frac{\hl{x^3}}{x^2}=x\ &\to\ x^3-2x^2+5x\ \to\ \ub{-5x^2+2x^2}{-3x^2}+\ub{11x-5x}{6x}-15\\ \frac{\hl{-3x^2}}{x^2}=-3\ &\to\ -3x^2+6x-15\ \to\ 0\\ x-3=0\ &\to\ x=3 \end{aligned}\]

Answer\(3,\ 1+2i,\ 1-2i\)

10
Solve: \(x^4-x^3-5x^2+3x+2=0\)
5 pts
\[\begin{aligned} 1-1-5+3+2&=0\ \to\ x=1\ \checkmark\\[4pt] \frac{\hl{x^4}}{x}=x^3\ &\to\ x^4-x^3\ \to\ 0x^3-5x^2\\ \frac{\hl{0x^3}}{x}=0x^2\ &\to\ 0\ \to\ -5x^2+3x\\ \frac{\hl{-5x^2}}{x}=-5x\ &\to\ -5x^2+5x\ \to\ \ub{3x-5x}{-2x}+2\\ \frac{\hl{-2x}}{x}=-2\ &\to\ -2x+2\ \to\ 0\\ \text{Left}:\ x^3-5x-2&\\[4pt] (\vl{-2})^3-5(\vl{-2})-2&=\ub{-8+10}{2}-2=0\ \to\ x=-2\ \checkmark\\[4pt] \frac{\hl{x^3}}{x}=x^2\ &\to\ x^3+2x^2\ \to\ -2x^2-5x\\ \frac{\hl{-2x^2}}{x}=-2x\ &\to\ -2x^2-4x\ \to\ \ub{-5x+4x}{-x}-2\\ \frac{\hl{-x}}{x}=-1\ &\to\ -x-2\ \to\ 0\\[4pt] x^2-2x-1&=0\\ x=\frac{2\pm\sqrt{\ub{4+4}{8}}}{2}&=\frac{2\pm\ub{\sqrt8}{2\sqrt2}}{2}=1\pm\sqrt2 \end{aligned}\]

Answer\(\{1,\ -2,\ 1+\sqrt2,\ 1-\sqrt2\}\)

11
Solve: \(x^4-5x^2-36=0\)
5 pts
\[\begin{aligned} \hl{-9}\cdot\hl4=-36,\ \ \hl{-9}+\hl4&=-5\\ (x^2-9)(x^2+4)&=0\\ x^2=9\ &\to\ x=\pm3\\ x^2=-4\ &\to\ x=\pm\sqrt{-4}=\pm2i \end{aligned}\]

Answer\(\{3,\ -3,\ 2i,\ -2i\}\)

Polynomials from their zeros

12
Find a 4th-degree polynomial with real coefficients: \(3i\) is a zero, \(1\) is a zero of multiplicity 2, and \(f(0)=18\).
5 pts
\[\begin{aligned} 3i\ &\to\ -3i\\ (x-3i)(x+3i)&=x^2-9\hl{i^2}=x^2+9\\ f(x)&=a(x^2+9)(x-1)^2\\ f(\vl0)&=a(9)(1)=9a=18\ \to\ a=2\\[4pt] (x^2+9)(x^2-2x+1)&=x^4-2x^3+\ub{x^2+9x^2}{10x^2}-18x+9\\ 2(x^4-2x^3+10x^2-18x+9)&=2x^4-4x^3+20x^2-36x+18 \end{aligned}\]

Answer\(f(x)=2x^4-4x^3+20x^2-36x+18\)

13
For \(f(x)=-x^5+4x^4-4x^3\): find each zero and its multiplicity, say whether the graph crosses or touches at each zero, and describe the end behavior.
5 pts
\[\begin{aligned} f(x)&=-x^3(\ub{x^2-4x+4}{(x-2)^2})=-x^{\hl3}(x-2)^{\hl2}\\ x=0:&\ \ \hl3\ \text{odd}\ \to\ \text{crosses}\\ x=2:&\ \ \hl2\ \text{even}\ \to\ \text{touches}\\ n=5\ \text{odd},\ a_n=-1\lt0\ &\to\ \nwarrow\ \cdots\ \searrow \end{aligned}\]

Answer\(0\): mult. 3, crosses · \(2\): mult. 2, touches and turns around · rises left, falls right

14
Find all zeros and write as a product of linear factors: \(f(x)=x^4-x^3+2x^2-4x-8\)
5 pts
\[\begin{aligned} f(\vl{-1})&=1+1+2+4-8=0\ \checkmark\\[4pt] \frac{\hl{x^4}}{x}=x^3\ &\to\ x^4+x^3\ \to\ \ub{-x^3-x^3}{-2x^3}+2x^2\\ \frac{\hl{-2x^3}}{x}=-2x^2\ &\to\ -2x^3-2x^2\ \to\ \ub{2x^2+2x^2}{4x^2}-4x\\ \frac{\hl{4x^2}}{x}=4x\ &\to\ 4x^2+4x\ \to\ \ub{-4x-4x}{-8x}-8\\ \frac{\hl{-8x}}{x}=-8\ &\to\ -8x-8\ \to\ 0\\[4pt] \ub{x^3-2x^2}{x^2(x-2)}+\ub{4x-8}{4(x-2)}&=(x-2)(x^2+4)\\ x^2+4=0\ \to\ x^2=-4\ &\to\ x=\pm2i \end{aligned}\]

AnswerZeros \(-1,\ 2,\ 2i,\ -2i\); \(f(x)=(x+1)(x-2)(x-2i)(x+2i)\)
